A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you say “what if?”

Can I play on my phone?

Your phone is perfect for this introduction, the looping demo, and sharing. The playable game currently needs a computer with a keyboard and mouse.

Can I write anything?

Write in any language supported by Google Gemini to change the environment’s supported rules. The ball, exit, and fixed layout stay in place. Each level shows what you can change.

Do I need all three stars to finish?

No. Reaching the exit passes the level. All three stars earn full score. Every level is open, and you can retry whenever you like.

Where does AI fit in?

AI interprets language; the engine runs the physics. The video records actual gameplay with scripted input. Development assistance and art credits are explained in the repository. Read the AI usage notes ↗
