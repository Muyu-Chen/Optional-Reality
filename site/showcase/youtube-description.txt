TITLE
Optional Reality — Change the Rules. Choose the Moment. | Gameplay Demo

DESCRIPTION
You don't control the ball. You change the rules of its world.

Optional Reality is a natural-language physics puzzle game. Write in any language supported by Google Gemini, review a supported rule change, and decide when to send it. Keep the momentum, redirect collisions, or power a little bakery toaster. Reach the exit; collect three stars to master the route.

This 47-second demo shows real recorded gameplay with scripted typing and three actual full-score finishes. The explicit recorded commands use the local parser. AI interprets freer language; the physics engine runs the simulation.
